# Design critique

**When to use**: Reviewing flows, components, microcopy, or rough mocks for usability and clarity.

## Method

1. **Audience & goal** — Who uses this and what success looks like.
2. **Hierarchy** — Primary action obvious? Secondary actions discoverable but calm?
3. **Accessibility** — Contrast, focus order, labels (not icon-only mystery meat).
4. **Copy** — Specific verbs; avoid jargon unless users expect it.
5. **Feedback** — Max three **must-fix**, then **nice-to-have**. Tie each to user impact.

Avoid purely subjective taste unless tied to measurable usability.
